# Privacy Policy

NEXTBASE values your privacy.

### 1. Data Collection
We collect minimal information such as email and transaction records only for licensing and support. Conversation data processed within the application may remain stored locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to our servers unless explicitly required for AI processing.

### 2. AI Processing
Some features may send user input to external AI services for processing in order to generate responses or translations. These services process data transiently and do not retain personal information beyond what is necessary to provide the service.

### 3. Payment Security
All payments are processed securely via Stripe or Gumroad. We do not store your credit card information on our servers.

### 4. Third-Party Disclosure
Your data will never be shared with third parties unless required by law.
